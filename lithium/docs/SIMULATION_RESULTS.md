Generated by `python -m sim.run_all` - 500 virtual patients, seed 2026, truth model `truth-perturbed`, engine prior `ensemble`, runtime 11.2 min.

These are simulations: they show whether the mechanisms work under stated assumptions, not clinical effectiveness. See docs/SIMULATION.md for methods and caveats.
